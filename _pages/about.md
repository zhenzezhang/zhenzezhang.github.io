Hi there! I'm a master's student and a member of Stanford's [Causality in Cognition Lab](https://cicl.stanford.edu/). I completed my undergraduate degree in Cognitive Sciences at UC Irvine, where I worked with [Dr. Ramesh Srinivasan](https://hnl.ss.uci.edu/) and [Dr. Emily Grossman](https://vpnl.ss.uci.edu/).

Research Interests
======

I am interested in using computational modeling to understand how people reason about the world, how they collaborate and communicate with others, and how they form judgments about other minds or intelligent systems.

Outside of Research
======

Outside of research, I enjoy reading, visiting museums, and fencing.
